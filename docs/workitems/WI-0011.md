Three small hardening items left open when WI-0003 was approved as mergeable. None is a defect in shipped behaviour; all three defend behaviour that already works.

(1) The bool(cap) guard in is_at_least_pct survives mutation. A header declaring "<=0 KB" really does parse to a (0, 0) cap, so the guard is reachable, but nothing pins it. Roughly a three-line test.

(2) The trailing-partial-line increment in the line count survives mutation. A 200-line file with no final newline would move from "over" to "approaching" if the increment were dropped. Also roughly three lines.

(3) The structured log record carries pct_of_cap: 100 next to level: "approaching" for files in the band just below the cap. That pairing is correct -- the percentage is rounded for humans, the level is decided exactly -- but it is not self-consistent on its face. Grepped 18.08.2026: no consumer re-derives the level from that field today, so nothing is broken. The risk is a future consumer, and the likely shape of one is a skill prompt that paraphrases the record rather than parsing it, which is exactly the reader that would reimport the rounding bug. Options: rename to pct_of_cap_rounded, carry the exact ratio alongside, or drop the field. Note that a test may assert the current field name, so this is not a free rename.
